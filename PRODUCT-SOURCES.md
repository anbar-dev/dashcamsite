## Shortlist rationale and editorial budget mapping

- **A119 Mini 2 — up to about $150:** single-channel entry option for people who prefer a compact front-only installation. Its HDR/frame-rate trade-off is now shown explicitly.
- **A229 Plus 2CH — up to about $250:** balanced front/rear option without a 4K front sensor. This is a guide classification, not a confirmed Amazon.com offer price.
- **70mai A510-1 — up to about $250:** two-channel value alternative with a lower-resolution rear camera. Check the U.S. listing for the rear camera, cable, and any bundled card.
- **A229 Plus 3CH and A229 Pro 2CH — up to about $350:** higher-tier cabin coverage or 4K-front dual coverage. These are guide classifications, not current price claims.
